## 1.0.0

`2026-10-27`

First release of Ayu Dawn, a fork of [ayu for VS Code](https://github.com/ayu-theme/vscode-ayu) 1.5.0.

- Mirage has paler backgrounds: `#363944` for the side bar, panels and tabs, `#242735` for the editor and terminal, and `#464951` for the title bar and status bar
- Mirage application text and activity bar icons are white
- Mirage Unbordered uses the same backgrounds as Mirage, with its dividers hidden
- Light is unchanged from ayu Light
- The Dark themes are not included
